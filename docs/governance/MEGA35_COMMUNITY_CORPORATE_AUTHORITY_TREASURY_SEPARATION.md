# Mega Step 35 — Community / Corporate Authority & Treasury Separation

Mega Step 35 establishes a fail-closed boundary between community governance
and legally consequential corporate authority.

## Canonical rules

1. Community membership is not legal citizenship.
2. Community governance does not create state authority or sovereignty.
3. Community governance may operate only within explicitly delegated platform
   or community scope.
4. Community approval cannot override fiduciary, statutory, regulatory,
   contractual, or other legally binding corporate duties.
5. Corporate legal decisions remain with appropriately authorized legal actors.
6. Platform treasury authority is not created by popularity, community status,
   token holdings, engagement, or informal approval.
7. Treasury actions require explicit authority, defined scope, documented
   purpose, and auditability.
8. No unbounded treasury authority is created.
9. No token-voting authority, election system, council, sovereignty layer, or
   private-beta activation is created by this Mega Step.
10. Existing protections from Mega Steps 27–34 remain authoritative.

The runtime boundary is an authorization and separation control. It is not an
election engine, treasury sovereignty mechanism, governmental system, or legal
citizenship framework.
