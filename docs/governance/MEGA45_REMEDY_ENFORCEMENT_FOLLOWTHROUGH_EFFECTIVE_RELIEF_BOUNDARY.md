# Mega Step 45 — Remedy Enforcement Follow-through & Effective Relief Boundary

Mega Step 45 closes the gap between granting a remedy and actually
effectuating it.

An appeal, review, reversal, restoration, or other remedy must not end as a
paper decision that is never carried out.

## Required follow-through evidence

A valid remedy follow-through requires:

1. the original consequential decision reference;
2. the appeal or remedy reference;
3. the relief that was actually granted;
4. an authorized remedy owner;
5. completed human review;
6. an explicit remedy state;
7. a bounded remedy scope;
8. the corrective action;
9. an audit reference;
10. an idempotency key;
11. an affected-subject reference;
12. privacy-boundary confirmation.

## Remedy lifecycle

Launch-time remedy states are explicit:

- pending;
- in progress;
- applied;
- completed;
- failed;
- partially applied;
- blocked.

Failed, partial, or blocked effectuation cannot disappear silently.
It must remain visible and must carry an escalation path.

## Effective relief

A granted remedy may not silently remain unenforced.

The corrective action may not exceed the relief granted by the underlying
review or appeal.

Restoration, reinstatement, reversal, or similar relief must expose an
explicit restoration state when required.

Repeated execution must be protected through idempotency and duplicate
effectuation must be rejected.

## Authority and rights safeguards

Consequential remedy execution remains human-review bound.

Wealth, popularity, follower count, economic power, or token balances do not
create remedy authority.

The boundary does not create governmental authority, statehood, legal
citizenship, sovereignty, or independent jurisdiction.

Privacy and security-sensitive evidence must not be exposed merely to satisfy
accountability requirements.

## Relationship to the durable moderation appeal system

Mega Step 45 does not replace the durable moderation appeal infrastructure
established earlier.

The existing moderation appeal persistence and review path remains intact.
Mega Step 45 adds the launch-time governance contract that requires granted
relief to progress to explicit, auditable, bounded, and effective
follow-through.

## Launch scope

This boundary does not add:

- a new database model;
- a migration;
- a new legal tribunal;
- automated legal judgments;
- governmental powers;
- secret remedy authority;
- automatic entitlement to relief beyond an authorized review outcome.
