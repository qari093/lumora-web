# Mega Step 38 — Constitutional Review & Ratification Boundary

Mega Step 38 converts Lumora's constitutional amendment requirements into an
executable review-and-ratification policy boundary.

## Required ratification chain

A constitutional amendment must fail closed unless all applicable safeguards
are satisfied:

1. authenticated consequential authority;
2. explicit delegation;
3. distinct current and proposed constitution versions;
4. documented amendment reason;
5. valid sealed amendment digest;
6. valid previous-version digest;
7. completed rights-impact review;
8. affirmative rights-impact approval;
9. independent review;
10. conflict disclosure and recusal where required;
11. completed review window;
12. configured approval threshold satisfied;
13. public change record prepared;
14. prior constitution version preserved;
15. valid effective date.

## Absolute launch-boundary prohibitions

The boundary does not permit:

- silent same-version constitutional rewriting;
- sole ratification by an affected power holder;
- reduction of Lumora's fundamental constitutional rights through this path;
- emergency bypass of ratification safeguards;
- caller-supplied authentication or delegation authority.

The authenticated API route derives consequential authority from Lumora's
canonical server-side admin-session boundary.

## Scope limitation

This Mega Step is a launch-time constitutional policy and reachability
boundary. It does **not** create a state, sovereign government, citizenship
system, public election system, token-voting system, legislature, or legal
jurisdiction.

It also does not claim that a durable ratification archive, public constitution
archive, governance election mechanism, or jurisdiction-specific legal overlay
has been implemented. Those remain separate capabilities unless independently
implemented and validated.

No database model or migration is required for this boundary.
