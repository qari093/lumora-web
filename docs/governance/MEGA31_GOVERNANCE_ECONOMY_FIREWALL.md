# Mega Step 31 — Governance–Economy Firewall and Authority Separation

Mega Step 31 operationalizes the constitutional rule that economic power,
token holdings, popularity, follower count, engagement, revenue, and
commercial status must not automatically create governance authority.

This boundary preserves the existing Lumora platform model:

- Lumora governance remains platform/community governance, not state authority.
- Governance authority must come from an explicit authenticated role or
  delegated governance authority.
- Zencoin holdings, wallet balances, revenue, purchases, follower counts,
  engagement scores, creator status, or commercial tier cannot independently
  create governance authority.
- Economic rewards and governance eligibility remain separate concepts.
- A user may be economically successful without holding governance authority.
- Governance authority may not be silently purchased or inferred from wealth.
- The boundary must fail closed when authority is absent.
- The authenticated authority inspection route must not expose private economic
  balances or sensitive account data.
- Mega Steps 27–30 remain intact: constitutional baseline, authority boundaries,
  due process/appeal/remedy, and transparent/auditable consequential decisions.
