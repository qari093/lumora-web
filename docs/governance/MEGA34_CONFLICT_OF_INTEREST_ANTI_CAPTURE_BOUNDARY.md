# Mega Step 34 — Conflict-of-Interest, Anti-Capture & Self-Dealing Authority Boundary

Mega Step 34 establishes the launch-baseline constitutional boundary preventing
consequential Lumora authority from being exercised through undisclosed
conflicts of interest, self-dealing, or unilateral control by a person whose
own powers or direct interests are affected.

## Required boundary

Consequential authority requires:

1. authenticated identity;
2. explicit delegated authority;
3. disclosure of relevant conflicts of interest;
4. recusal where the deciding actor is conflicted;
5. independent review where a material conflict exists;
6. no self-review or direct self-dealing;
7. no unilateral constitutional veto merely because the actor's own power is affected.

The boundary does not create a new council, election system, token-voting
system, treasury sovereignty mechanism, state authority, or private-beta
activation mechanism.

## Anti-capture guarantees

Popularity, wealth, Zencoin, commercial status, follower count, engagement,
emergency status, AI recommendations, or administrative role alone do not
erase conflict-of-interest safeguards.

An authenticated administrator remains subject to the same anti-self-dealing
boundary for consequential decisions.

## Scope

This gate provides a canonical fail-closed decision primitive and authenticated
inspection surface. Existing Mega Steps 27–33 remain authoritative and are not
weakened or reopened.
