# Mega Step 37 — Rights Impact Review Boundary

Mega Step 37 creates an executable launch boundary for rights-impact review
before consequential governance actions may proceed.

The boundary requires:

1. authenticated consequential authority;
2. explicit delegation;
3. a defined action and legitimate aim;
4. identification of affected rights;
5. evidence supporting the intervention;
6. demonstrated necessity;
7. rejection where a less restrictive alternative remains available;
8. proportionality;
9. safeguards appropriate to the impact;
10. human review for consequential impacts;
11. appeal or remedy;
12. time limits and review dates where required;
13. transparency;
14. conflict-of-interest review and independent review where necessary.

The launch boundary fails closed against reduction of fundamental rights,
silent rights restrictions, indefinite restrictions, and automated final
approval of consequential rights-impact decisions.

This Mega Step does not create state authority, nationality, legal
sovereignty, a court, token voting, treasury authority, or an autonomous
governance actor.

It also does not claim that this review record is durably archived in a
governance database. Durable publication/archive infrastructure remains a
separate capability from this executable decision boundary.
