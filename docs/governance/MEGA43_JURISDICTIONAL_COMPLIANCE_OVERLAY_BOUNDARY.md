# Mega Step 43 — Jurisdictional Compliance Overlay and Constitutional Core Protection

This launch boundary permits jurisdiction-specific lawful requirements or stronger protections while preserving Lumora's constitutional protection floor. A valid overlay requires a named jurisdiction, documented applicable-law basis, bounded scope, rights-impact review, and a public accountability reference where safe.

The overlay cannot silently erase fundamental rights, due process, privacy, accountability, human review, remedy or appeal. It cannot create governmental status, statehood, legal citizenship, legal nationality, or independent jurisdictional authority. Lumora remains a platform/service/company ecosystem subject to applicable law and legally authorized corporate actors.

This is an application-architecture safeguard, not legal advice or country-by-country legal certification. It does not claim a complete legal database or automated determination of applicable law.
