# Mega Step 44 — Constitutional Conflict Resolution & Precedence Boundary

Mega Step 44 creates the executable launch-time boundary for conflicts between
governance rules, jurisdictional overlays, delegated authority, emergency
authority, automation, economic authority, constitutional processes, privacy,
public accountability, and other platform governance requirements.

## Required conflict evidence

A conflict evaluation requires:

1. a description of the conflict;
2. both rule sources;
3. the applicable-law context;
4. the affected rule scope;
5. completed rights-impact review;
6. confirmation that the conflict can be safely resolved;
7. a public accountability reference where safe.

## Non-negotiable precedence protections

Conflict resolution may not silently remove or reduce:

- the fundamental-rights floor;
- due process;
- privacy;
- required human review;
- remedy and appeal;
- accountability.

Applicable law cannot simply be ignored by a platform governance rule.

Emergency authority cannot gain unlimited precedence. Delegated authority cannot
exceed its parent scope. Wealth, popularity, follower count, economic power, or
token balances do not create constitutional precedence.

Unsafe or genuinely unresolved conflicts fail closed instead of being silently
resolved in favor of the more powerful actor.

## Legal and institutional boundary

This mechanism does not create governmental authority, legal citizenship,
statehood, sovereignty, or independent jurisdiction. Lumora remains a platform,
service, and company ecosystem subject to applicable law and its legally
authorized corporate actors.

This boundary is an application-architecture safeguard. It is not legal advice,
a country-by-country legal database, automated legal interpretation, or a
substitute for qualified legal review.

## Launch scope

This Mega Step intentionally does not add:

- a new database model;
- a migration;
- automated legal advice;
- automated determination of applicable law;
- a universal country-by-country regulatory rules engine;
- governmental or judicial powers;
- secret constitutional precedence rules.
