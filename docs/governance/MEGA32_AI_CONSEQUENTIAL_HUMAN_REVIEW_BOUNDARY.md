# Mega Step 32 — AI Automation & Consequential Human-Review Boundary

Mega Step 32 establishes the launch-runtime boundary between automated analysis and consequential human authority.

## Locked runtime rules

1. AI, scoring, trust, ranking, moderation, or other automation may analyze, recommend, rank, or create temporary safety gates.
2. Automated trust or reputation scores do not create governance authority.
3. Automated decisions may not become irreversible consequential sanctions without a human-review path.
4. Temporary automated safety gates remain explicitly non-final.
5. Remedy remains available for consequential moderation decisions.
6. Automation may not secretly alter governance rights.
7. Automation may not secretly manipulate reputation.
8. Human-reviewed consequential action requires an explicit authorized human path.
9. Mega Steps 27–31 constitutional, authority, due-process, audit, and economy-firewall protections remain intact.
10. This gate creates no new council, election, sovereignty, treasury authority, token voting, or private-beta activation.

Existing route status behavior is preserved where possible. The added constitutional metadata distinguishes advisory analysis and temporary safety gates from final consequential authority.
