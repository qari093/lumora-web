# LCE-X+ Anti-Goals and Legal Boundaries

Status: LOCKED
Step Lock: 002/375

## Anti-Goals
- Do not present unverified rumors as facts.
- Do not imply ownership, licensing, or distribution rights that Lumora does not have.
- Do not bypass region, age, rights, or source restrictions.
- Do not optimize purely for addiction at the expense of safety, trust, or legality.
- Do not show dead, broken, or misleading teaser cards when safer fallback is available.
- Do not allow unofficial low-trust sources to outrank official sources by default.
- Do not publish derivative transformations that violate rights, safety, or cultural controls.
- Do not create dark-pattern engagement loops around countdowns, scarcity, or false exclusivity.

## Legal Boundaries
- Official-source-first display policy is mandatory.
- Rights-aware rendering is mandatory.
- Metadata-only fallback must be used whenever media display rights are unclear or unavailable.
- Region restrictions must be respected.
- Age-safety restrictions must be respected.
- Auditability of source and display decisions must be preserved.
- Manual review paths must exist for uncertain, controversial, or culturally sensitive cases.
- User-facing explainability must not conflict with compliance obligations.

## Product Rule
Trust, legality, and cultural safety outrank short-term engagement.
